Game support

Get help with your journey on iPhone and iPad.

What does the purchase unlock?

Play Chapter 1 for free. A single purchase unlocks Chapters 2–6 across all three journey slots. The unlock screen shows your local App Store price.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought the unlock. From the game’s title screen, tap Restore purchase, complete the grown-up check and follow Apple’s prompts. You’ll need a connection to reach Apple. Restoring the purchase gives you chapter access. Saved story progress stays on the device where you played.

How do I continue or start over?

Choose Continue Journey to return to a saved adventure. For a second player, choose New Journey and pick an empty slot. Choosing an occupied slot asks you to confirm before replacing it.

Contact support

For help with Ram Leela, email ramleela.support@gmail.com. Include the chapter, what happened, your device model and the game version if available. Please keep passwords and payment details out of your message.
