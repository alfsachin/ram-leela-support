Privacy policy

Ram Leela is an offline story game. Its developers collect no personal data through the game. There are no player accounts, ads, trackers or developer analytics.

Your journeys

Your device stores your journeys, story choices, settings and chapter-unlock status. The game does not send these to the developers. You can replace a saved journey with New Journey or delete the app to remove its local data. Apple separately manages any device backups you choose to keep.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and restores. The game receives the transaction information needed to unlock the chapters you own. The developers do not receive your payment card details through the game. Apple manages your App Store purchase history.

Share and Rate

If you tap Share, you decide where to send the game invitation using Apple’s share sheet. It contains the game’s name and store link. Your saved journey stays on your device. Tapping Rate opens the App Store. Apple and any service you choose apply their own privacy policies.

Support and privacy requests

The support page links to a public issue tracker on GitHub. If you choose to post, your GitHub username and report are public. Keep personal information out of your report. GitHub manages its website under its own privacy policy. There is no player account to delete.
